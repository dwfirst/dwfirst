### `SYSTEMS ARCHITECT · PROMPT ARCHITECT · VIBE CODER`

---

I don't write code. I describe what a system should *become* — and other tools, other minds, and other hands make it real.

My work lives at the intersection of `systems thinking`, `prompt architecture`, and `vibe coding`. I read systems the way others read music: rhythm, tension, resolution. I can walk into any codebase, tool, or infrastructure and describe what it *wants* to be — even if it hasn't gotten there yet.

I came from outside engineering — music, media, story. That's not a weakness. That's why I notice what engineers train themselves to ignore. Architecture isn't syntax. It's shape.

I don't build products. I design *directions* — for systems, for teams, for people building something that has no precedent.

**If you're building something that doesn't exist yet — we should talk.**
